## The Trend You Thought Was Going One Way Just Reversed

For most of this decade, the wellness industry told a tidy story: consumers were cutting sweeteners, clean labels were winning, and the direction of travel was obvious. The EU-27 data says that story has an asterisk — and if you're running a coaching business or a fitness studio, you need to read the footnote carefully.

Sweetener consumption in Europe fell every single year from 2018 to 2022. Then it turned. It has risen every year since, and the forward projections show no sign of flattening. This is not noise. This is a confirmed inflection.

## What the Numbers Actually Tell You

The per-capita figure is the one to watch. It strips out population growth and shows what individuals are actually doing. It troughed at 5.47 kg in 2022 and has climbed steadily since — reaching 5.68 kg in 2024, heading to 5.82 kg in 2025, and projected to hit 6.2 kg by 2028. That endpoint is higher than where we started in 2018 (5.7 kg). Europeans, on average, will be consuming more sweetener per person than at any point in this dataset.

Total volume tells the same story at scale. The market is on course for 2.75 billion kg by 2028 — a full recovery from the 2.43 billion kg low recorded in 2022. The dip happened. It is now substantially over.

The critical question isn't whether this is happening — the data is clear. The question is *why*, and the data doesn't answer that directly. It could reflect a shift toward functional sweeteners and sugar alternatives rather than sugar itself. It could reflect consumer fatigue with ultra-restrictive eating. Likely it is both. Either way, the behavioural reality your clients are living in is one of increasing sweetener presence, not decreasing.

## What Operators Should Do With This

First, **update your narrative**. Any content, handout, or sales conversation built on the premise that sweeteners are fading from European diets is now outdated. Clients who do their own research will notice the discrepancy, and credibility is your most fragile asset.

Second, **get ahead of the product wave**. A market climbing from 2.52 billion kg in 2024 toward 2.75 billion kg in 2028 is a market that food and supplement brands are actively targeting. Your clients will be marketed to aggressively. The operator who helps them navigate that landscape — through education, label literacy, and honest coaching — becomes indispensable. The operator who ignores it becomes irrelevant.

Third, **reframe your nutrition coaching offer**. Avoidance-based frameworks are hard to sustain when the category you're telling people to avoid is growing in their everyday environment. Pragmatic, literacy-based guidance travels further — and it positions you as a realist, not a purist.
