## The Growth Curve Is Telling You Something

Probiotics had their moment of explosive expansion. Between 2017 and 2021, the global supplement market in this category climbed from $109.5M to $160.7M — a period bookended by rising wellness awareness and turbocharged by pandemic-driven immunity anxiety. That was fast money and fast adoption.

The curve has not reversed. It is bending. The forecast out to 2027 still points upward, ending at $224.1M. But the annual increments are shrinking. That deceleration is not a warning sign for the category — it is a structural signal about where you sit as an operator.

## What Deceleration Actually Means for Your Business

When a supplement category is still growing but slowing, two things happen simultaneously. First, major consumer brands pour in marketing spend to defend share — which actually increases client awareness and curiosity. Second, the product itself starts to feel generic. Both of those dynamics are useful to you, if you act before the second one dominates.

Right now, your clients are being reached by probiotic advertising but are largely unguided on what to actually take, when, and why. A market sitting at $189.2M in 2023 is not a niche — it is a category where real consumer dollars are already in motion. The question is whether those dollars come with any connection to your coaching or your community.

They will not by accident.
