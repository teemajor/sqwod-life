## The Category Nobody's Rushing Into — Yet

The question for coaches and studio owners isn't *whether* to engage this category. It's *when* and *how*.

## What the Curve Actually Says

The growth from 2017 to 2027 tells two stories depending on where you look. Annual gains are still positive, but the pace is moderating. This is a category entering a maturation phase, not a collapse — but it does mean the window for differentiated positioning is narrowing.

You're contextualizing — connecting a product they've heard of to outcomes they care about: recovery, digestion, energy consistency, immune resilience.
