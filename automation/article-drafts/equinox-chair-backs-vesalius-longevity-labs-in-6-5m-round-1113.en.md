## Harvey Spevak puts $6.5M behind a peptide and longevity platform

According to [Athletech News](https://athletechnews.com/equinox-chair-harvey-spevak-leads-6-5-million-financing-round-vesalius-longevity-labs/), Vesalius Longevity Labs has closed a $6.5 million private placement led by an investor group organised by Harvey Spevak, chairman of luxury fitness and wellness giant Equinox Group. The company sold approximately 6.2 million units at $1.05 each, generating nearly $6.5 million in gross proceeds. Vesalius, formerly known as Sweet Earth Holdings Corporation and publicly traded, operates a provider-focused platform connecting licensed healthcare providers with peptide therapies, biologics, preventive healthcare and performance optimisation services. The capital will fund sales team expansion, new market entry and platform growth, Athletech News reported.

Spevak framed the investment in broad terms, calling longevity "one of the most exciting opportunities in health and wellness over the coming decade" and pointing to his experience building consumer-focused brands as the value he brings to the company's next phase.

## Why gym and studio operators should watch the longevity money trail

This deal sits inside a wider pattern of fitness capital moving toward longevity and performance health. The Sqwod Deal Tracker shows Equinox itself raised more than $1 billion in November 2026, giving its leadership significant firepower to explore adjacent bets. Spevak's personal involvement in Vesalius suggests Equinox's orbit is actively scouting longevity infrastructure, not just wellness amenities. For mid-market and independent gym operators, that matters: the services Vesalius supplies, peptides, biologics and performance optimisation, are increasingly what high-value members expect to access through or alongside their fitness provider. Operators who have not yet considered how longevity services fit their member offer are watching the premium end of the market accelerate away from them. What remains unknown is whether Vesalius intends to partner directly with fitness facilities or will grow exclusively through licensed healthcare providers outside the gym channel.

## Sqwod's take: the chairman's cheque tells you where the premium market is heading

When the chairman of the world's most recognisable luxury gym brand personally leads a financing round for a peptide and longevity company, that is not a casual side investment. It is a signal about where Equinox-calibre members are spending and what they expect their wellness relationships to include. Luxury operators who can credibly bridge fitness and longevity services stand to deepen member retention and revenue per visit. Everyone else risks becoming the commodity layer underneath a richer longevity ecosystem they do not control. The winners here are likely to be platforms, like Vesalius, that own the provider network, and the fitness brands senior enough to plug into it on favourable terms. Smaller independent studios are the most exposed: they lack the scale to negotiate partnerships and the brand weight to position longevity services convincingly on their own.
