## The money is moving. Are you?

Venture capital is pouring into wellness. SuperMush just closed a $3M Series A to scale its functional mushroom line, per Dealroom. Viome, a Bellevue-based wellness company, just made an acquisition to broaden its health offerings, according to The Business Journals. Italy is winning the World Spa Awards 2026 cycle, with Luxus Plus reporting that spas are redefining what premium wellness even means. The category is hot, investor attention is real, and consumer appetite is growing.

Meanwhile, the average fitness operator is leaving the front door wide open and forgetting to greet anyone who walks through it.

## The embarrassing truth hiding in your CRM

Here's the gut-punch: according to Athletech News citing ATN Insights, **88% of leads receive no follow-up within 72 hours**. Nearly nine out of ten people who raise their hand and say *I want to give you money* hear nothing back in the window when they're most likely to convert.

This isn't a marketing problem. It's a discipline problem. And it's happening at exactly the moment when the broader wellness market is getting more competitive, not less. body LIFE's coverage of all inclusive Fitness notes that expansion is accelerating across the European fitness market — more studios, more formats, more options for the consumer who didn't hear back from you.

When supply grows and follow-up stays broken, you don't just lose a lead. You fund your competitor's new member.

Smart glasses are entering the gym floor too — DSSV covers the emerging use of wearable tech in fitness studio environments — which means the *experience* side of fitness is getting a serious upgrade. Operators are investing in hardware, atmospherics, and programming. That's fine. But no amount of biometric feedback equipment saves a business that can't send a follow-up message.

## What to actually do this week

The opportunity here is embarrassingly simple, which is why most people won't take it.

**Fix the 72-hour window first.** Audit your lead flow today. Map every touchpoint from inquiry to first human contact. If that gap is longer than a few hours, you have a revenue leak that no new equipment or spa-style rebrand will fix.

- Set an automated acknowledgment that fires within minutes of a lead coming in — not a generic blast, something that references what they asked about.
- Assign a real person to follow up personally within 24 hours. Not a sequence. A person.
- Build a three-touch cadence over 72 hours: message, call, message. Then stop and move on. Respect their time and yours.

The wellness brands attracting capital right now — functional nutrition players, longevity-focused platforms, experience-led spas — are winning because they've connected a compelling product to a reliable customer journey. The product alone doesn't close anyone.

You don't need a $3M raise or an award-winning spa floor to compete. You need to call your leads back. Start there.
