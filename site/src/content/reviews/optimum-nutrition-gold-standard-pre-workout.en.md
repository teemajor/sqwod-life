Most of what a pre-workout does comes from two ingredients with strong evidence: caffeine and creatine. ON's Gold Standard Pre-Workout gets both right: 175 mg caffeine, under the 200 mg single dose EFSA considers safe for healthy adults, and 3.4 g creatine, a full daily dose.

The pump side is lighter than research doses, and like nearly every booster it is sweetened. German food inspectors have found problem substances in a large share of pre-workouts, so a big brand with a clear label is worth something here.
