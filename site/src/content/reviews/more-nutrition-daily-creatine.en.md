There is nothing wrong with what is in the tub: creatine monohydrate at the studied 3 g dose. The issue is what you pay for it. At roughly 37 cents per serving, the 250 g tub costs several times Bulk or Optimum Nutrition for the same molecule.

MORE says its products are regularly lab-analysed, but we found no Informed Sport or Kölner Liste listing for this product, and the crowd signal is the brand's shop rating rather than reviews of this creatine.
