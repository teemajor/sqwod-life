Hydraid is one of the best-selling electrolyte sticks on Amazon.de, and buyers like it. German testers do not: ÖKO-TEST rated it "mangelhaft" in issue 7/2025, and its method marks down exactly what Hydraid adds, extra vitamins and zinc, plus mineral levels above German guidance.

We publish low scores too. The product is safe and tested (it is on the Kölner Liste), but as a hydration drink it carries ingredients you do not need and costs about 80 cents a stick for them.
