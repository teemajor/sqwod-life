RENPHO's gun is the default answer on Amazon.de, with over 35,000 ratings averaging 4.4★. At 0.68 kg it is easy to hold, and at around €65 it costs a third of a Hypervolt.

Two caveats. It was not part of the Stiftung Warentest test, which found high levels of naphthalene in two branded guns, so there is no independent check on materials. And the headline 3,200 rpm matters less than the low end: the testers note muscles relax below about 30 beats a second, so use the lower settings.
