Most electrolyte brands race to the biggest sodium number. PILLAR went the other way: 400 mg per serve, mixed into 500 to 750 ml, which puts it right inside the range German testers consider correct for a sport drink and keeps the drink hypotonic.

The catch is logistics. PILLAR's Munich-based EU shop sells its magnesium and creatine but not this product yet, so it ships from Australia, and the only reviews are on PILLAR's own shop. We marked confidence Low and will re-score when it lands in the EU.
