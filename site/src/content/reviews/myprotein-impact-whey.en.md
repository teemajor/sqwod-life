Impact Whey is the default value protein: a whey concentrate at roughly €30/kg with 50+ flavours, Informed Choice and Informed Protein certification, and a deep pool of strong ratings. For a coach recommending an affordable, tested daily protein, it is the obvious pick.

Two caveats keep it honest. It is a concentrate, so it carries more lactose and carbs than an isolate, and taste swings by flavour. And the headline value depends on Myprotein’s near-constant discount codes — at full RRP it is far less compelling.
