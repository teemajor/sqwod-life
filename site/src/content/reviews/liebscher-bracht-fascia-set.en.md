Liebscher & Bracht is the other big name in German fascia training, with a following built on pain-therapy programmes. This set pairs a roller and a ball, each with a soft surface over a harder core, so you can lean in without the sharp pressure of a firm roller.

The products are well made and well loved. Their marketing makes bigger promises about pain than the research supports: ÖKO-TEST rated the evidence for foam rolling as a therapy "not very convincing". Buy it to feel looser, not to fix a diagnosis.
