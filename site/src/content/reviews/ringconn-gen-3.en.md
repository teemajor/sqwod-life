The RingConn Gen 3 opened for pre-order on 5 May 2026 and launched on 29 May. It keeps the Gen 2's no-subscription model and adds a vibration motor for health, inactivity and low-battery alerts, a vascular trend feature that combines cuff calibrations with ring data, upgraded heart-rate and temperature sensors and a universal wireless charging case. RingConn rates the battery at 11-14 days; netzwelt.de got close to 13 days and rated it 1,8 (gut), and Gadgets & Wearables scored it 8.6/10.

The limits haven't moved much. Exercise heart rate still lags, Notebookcheck measured cuff-calibrated blood pressure readings 4-6 mmHg lower than a cuff (treat them as trends), and the app is still less polished than Oura's. The motor makes the ring 2.3 mm thick (Gen 2: 2.0 mm), and the price rises to 369 EUR in Germany. TechRadar called it a refinement rather than a big upgrade.

Compared with our [RingConn Gen 2 review](/en/verified/ringconn-gen-2), the app score inches up, while comfort and value come down a little.
