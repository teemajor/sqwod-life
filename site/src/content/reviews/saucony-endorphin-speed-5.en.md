The Endorphin Speed 5 keeps the PWRRUN PB foam and nylon plate that made the line a favourite, with a reshaped, more flexible plate, more heel rubber and a new upper. RunRepeat scores it 94/100 and measured energy return close to super-shoe level, while Doctors of Running found the forefoot no longer bottoms out on long runs.

The trade-offs are small but real: it is slightly heavier and less agile than the Speed 4, and RunRepeat lists a durability downgrade. Saucony's German store lists it at €160, down from an RRP of €200.

Compared with the [Endorphin Speed 4](/en/verified/saucony-endorphin-speed-4), the 5 is softer, bouncier and roomier in the toe box, but a little heavier and currently more expensive.
