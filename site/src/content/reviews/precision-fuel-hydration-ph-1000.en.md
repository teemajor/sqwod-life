Precision Fuel & Hydration built its name with triathletes and ultra runners by treating sodium as something to match to the athlete, not maximise. PH 1000 is the middle strength: one tablet in 500 ml gives 1,000 mg sodium per litre, at the top of but still inside the range German testers accept for sport drinks.

It is batch tested against the WADA list and hypotonic. The trade-off is price: about one euro per tablet makes it the dearest serving in this ranking.
