Suunto announced the Race 2 on 27 August 2025, from 499 EUR in steel and 599 EUR in titanium. It keeps the 49 mm format but moves to a 1.5-inch LTPO AMOLED at 2,000 nits, slims down to 12.5 mm and 76 g (65 g in titanium), and stretches dual-band GPS to up to 55 hours. Free offline maps, ZoneSense, climb guidance and new running metrics such as ground contact time and vertical oscillation are on board, and Suunto redesigned the case back and optical heart-rate system.

That last change matters most. TechRadar calls the heart-rate accuracy much improved and scored it 4/5 on value and 4.5/5 on design, features and performance; Tom's Guide still saw a couple of runs that were well off and recommends a chest strap. Stiftung Warentest graded it 2,3 (gut). Smart features stay thin, and at around 449 EUR in Germany it now costs roughly 150 EUR more than the original Race.

Compared with our [Suunto Race review](/en/verified/suunto-race), display, battery and training features move up, while value comes down.
