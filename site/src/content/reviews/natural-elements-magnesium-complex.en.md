This is the magnesium most German Amazon shoppers end up with, and the reviews are excellent. The problem is the label: two capsules a day add up to 400 mg, the kind of dose ÖKO-TEST criticised in half the products it tested in June 2026, because it sits above the 250 mg the BfR considers the safe ceiling from supplements.

The fix is simple, because the dose splits: one capsule gives 200 mg. Used that way it is a good-value daily magnesium. We score the product as labelled, though, which is why it sits below Sunday Natural and Abtei.
