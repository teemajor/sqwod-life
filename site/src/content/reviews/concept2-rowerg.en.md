The Concept2 RowErg has been the reference indoor rower for decades, and the data backs the reputation: near-perfect scores from owners on Amazon.de (about 4.8/5 across thousands of ratings) plus top marks from independent testers like Garage Gym Reviews (4.6/5) and TreadmillReviewGuru (92/100). Air resistance with an adjustable damper gives a natural, scalable stroke, while the PM5 monitor delivers the accurate, trusted metrics that gyms and rowing clubs standardise on. Bluetooth and ANT+ let you sync heart rate and export workouts through the free ErgData app.

The trade-offs are physical rather than functional. It is long (roughly 2.4 m) and the flywheel is genuinely loud, so it is not ideal for a shared apartment or early-morning sessions next to sleeping housemates. There is no built-in touchscreen or subscription class library. But it separates into two parts for storage, holds its value on the used market better than almost anything else, and Concept2's fixed pricing means you rarely overpay. For most buyers it is a buy-once, keep-forever machine.
