Stiftung Warentest put 11 massage guns through physiotherapist trials, a 500-session endurance test and a lab check for harmful substances in July 2024. None scored "good". The BLACKROLL Fascia Gun came out on top at 2.7, praised for its massage and handling, and its pressing force was rated "very good".

It is a compact gun, which is the point: the testers found the deepest, hardest-hitting guns too strong and even painful. If you want something you will actually use on the neck and shoulders, this is the safest pick. If you want deep work on quads and glutes, a full-size gun goes further.
