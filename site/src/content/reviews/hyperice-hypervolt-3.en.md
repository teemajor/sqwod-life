Hyperice launched the Hypervolt 3 line on 10 March 2026, with the Hypervolt 3 sitting between the Go 3 and the 3 Pro. The standard model gets five speeds from 1,500 to 2,500 rpm, a stated 60 lb stall force, up to four hours of battery, a built-in pressure sensor that shows how hard you are pushing, and five redesigned heads that are 33% larger, including a Heated Head. It weighs 0.91 kg and keeps Bluetooth guidance through the Hyperice app, which is what makes it easy to hand to clients.

Hyperice's European list price is €249, and Hyperice now sells it on Amazon.de for €199. The crowd picture is thin: 58 Amazon.de ratings at 4.3★, 4.6/5 across 761 Trustpilot reviews at brand level, and one Coolblue product review at 7.5/10. The Hypervolt 2 drew a Stiftung Warentest "mangelhaft", and the new model has no German test result yet, so confidence is Low.

Against the [Hypervolt 2](/en/verified/hyperice-hypervolt-2), it adds two speeds, about an hour of battery, a pressure sensor and a heated head, and at €199 it now costs less than the Hypervolt 2's €219.
