SiS GO Hydro has been a staple of British cycling since 2008 and still makes sense today: one tablet in 500 ml gives about 690 mg sodium per litre, Informed Sport tests every batch, and it costs around 35 cents a serving.

We marked down the formula for sucralose and the added B vitamins, which do nothing for hydration and are exactly what German testers criticise. On Amazon.de it is sold by a third-party reseller, so our link goes to SiS directly.
