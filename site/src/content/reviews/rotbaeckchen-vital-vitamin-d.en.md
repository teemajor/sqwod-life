Stiftung Warentest tested 25 vitamin D products for its January 2026 issue and recommended only two. This is one of them, because it does what the testers wanted: 400 IU a day, well inside the BfR's 800 IU guidance, with clear labelling.

The trade-offs come with the juice format. You pay about 89 cents a day, the base is apple and mango juice, and the bottle has to live in the fridge. Our value score reflects that.
