The Forerunner 170 (announced in May 2026, on sale from 15 May) uses the same 43 mm case, 1.2-inch AMOLED and Elevate Gen 4 heart-rate sensor as the 165 and spends the upgrade on software. Training Readiness, Training Status with acute load, wrist-based running power and running dynamics, cycling power support and Garmin Coach plans now sit at the entry level, along with Garmin Pay and a gyroscope. the5krunner scored it 86%, TechRadar 4/5, and testbericht.de lists 90/100 from two tests.

The compromises are the ones you'd expect at this price: single-band GPS, the previous-generation optical sensor (weakest in upper-body gym work), no maps and no triathlon mode. Battery is rated at up to 10 days, a little behind the 165. The RRP rose to 299.99 EUR, but German street prices now sit around 250 EUR, about where the 165 was when we reviewed it.

Compared with our [Garmin Forerunner 165 review](/en/verified/garmin-forerunner-165), coach fit and experience move up thanks to the new training metrics, while value and battery dip slightly.
