Loop Quiet 2 is the sleep-focused plug in Loop’s lineup, and it’s an easy recommendation: 24 dB SNR passive reduction, four tip sizes for a reliable seal, and a low, flat profile that reviewers single out as comfortable for side sleepers — all reusable and washable for ~€20. Across 13,500+ on-site reviews it sits at 4.2/5, with an independent lab “B” grade for reduction.

Just calibrate expectations. It’s comfort-and-quiet, not a vault: the marketed 24 dB SNR measures closer to ~21 dB by independent NRR standards, so it takes the edge off noise rather than erasing loud snoring. For a cheap, comfy, reusable sleep and focus aid, it’s among the best value in the category.
