Launched on 10 November 2025 at 269 EUR, the COROS PACE 4 keeps what made the PACE 3 our value pick and fixes its weakest point: the display is now a 1.2-inch AMOLED at 1,500 nits. It still weighs about 32 g on the nylon band, COROS rates it for up to 19 days of daily use and 41 hours of GPS, and DC Rainmaker, Wareable and Tom's Guide all found GPS and heart rate reliable for running. A new action button, a microphone for voice notes and, since COROS's September 2026 update, offline maps (still in beta) round it out.

It is still a training tool first. There are no NFC payments and no music streaming, and Wareable and DC Rainmaker saw heart rate struggle in some non-running sessions, so a chest strap still helps for intervals and gym work. The bigger question is price: around 268 EUR at German retailers versus roughly 199 EUR for the PACE 3.

Compared with our [COROS PACE 3 review](/en/verified/coros-pace-3), accuracy and features move up, and value comes down because the new model costs about a third more.
