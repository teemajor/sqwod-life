Peloton launched its Cross Training series in October 2025, and the Cross Training Bike replaces the original Bike as the entry model: Peloton's US store now sells the original Bike only refurbished. The main hardware change at this tier is a 21.5-inch Full HD touchscreen that rotates 360°, so one machine covers rides and floor sessions. It keeps manual magnetic resistance and a 17 kg flywheel, takes riders up to 135 kg and needs about 137 x 58 cm of floor.

The cost model is unchanged, and it got pricier. Fitshop sells the bike in Germany for €1,899, and the All-Access Membership you need for classes is now €45 a month (it was €39), covering the whole household. Over three years that is about €3,519, against roughly €2,604 for the original Bike. Crowd signal is mostly platform-wide: the Peloton app holds 4.9 on the App Store (818k ratings) and 4.0 on Google Play, while Peloton's Trustpilot score sits at 2.8 across 6,761 reviews. T3 gave the Cross Training Bike+ its Platinum award, but we found no independent review of the base model.

Compared with the [original Peloton Bike](/en/verified/peloton-bike), you get the swivel screen for about €700 more up front and €6 more a month.
