The Eight Sleep Pod 6 launched on 23 September 2026 and is sold in Germany directly through Eight Sleep. The core idea is unchanged: a water-cooled cover that cools or heats each side of the bed between 13°C and 43°C and tracks heart rate, HRV, breathing rate, snoring and sleep stages without a wearable. This generation rebuilds the hardware: the hub is roughly half the size and fits under most bed frames, temperature changes run up to 20% faster, and the cover now carries 18 biometric sensors, up from 2. It still works with the existing Blanket, Pillow Cover and Base, sold separately.

The economics improved, but this is still a big spend. A Solo cover for a 90 x 200 cm bed costs €2,199, two-person versions run from €2,899 (135 x 190 cm) to €3,399 (200 x 200 cm), and Autopilot is mandatory at €199, €299 or €399 a year. Because the product is only weeks old, crowd data is thin: our crowd score rests on Eight Sleep's brand-level Trustpilot rating (3.4/5 across 1,053 reviews), so confidence is Low until independent long-term reviews land. Eight Sleep has filed for FDA clearance of its sleep apnea detection, so treat that feature as pending.

Compared with the [Pod 5](/en/verified/eight-sleep-pod-5), Pod 6 adds nine times the sensors, a smaller and faster hub and a Solo size, at a lower entry price.
