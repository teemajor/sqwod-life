DRYLL is a young German brand doing the high-salt thing properly: 1,000 mg sodium, 300 mg potassium and 60 mg of well-absorbed magnesium per stick, sweetened with stevia and nothing else, and listed on the Kölner Liste.

Our one caution is the dose. Mixed as directed into 500 ml, a stick lands around 2,000 mg sodium per litre, well above the 1,150 mg ceiling German testers apply to sport drinks. That suits heavy, salty sweaters in long sessions; for a normal gym hour, one stick per litre is the smarter way to use it.
