The Philips Somneo HF3650 sits at the top of Philips' wake-up light range and remains one of the most recommended sunrise alarms in Germany. Over the last half hour before your alarm the light gradually brightens from a deep red through orange to a bright warm-white, mimicking a natural sunrise so your body wakes more gently than it would to a buzzer. At night the RelaxBreathe function pulses a soft light you breathe along with, and a sunset mode dims the room to help you drift off. Reviewers consistently praise the smooth light and colour transitions and the wide range of customisation.

The trade-offs are price and audio. At roughly €190-200 it is a premium purchase, and the built-in speaker is the unit's weakest link, sounding thin at higher volumes. There is no app or wireless connectivity either, so everything is controlled from the on-device buttons. Still, with a 4.4/5 average across 1,580 Amazon ratings, 8.7/10 at Coolblue and a solid test score, it is a dependable, well-liked choice for anyone whose main goal is waking up more naturally.
