Waterdrop is the Austrian brand that popularised the 'microdrink': a thumb-sized cube you drop into 400–600ml of still or sparkling water to flavour it without sugar. Each cube is sweetened with stevia and carries fruit and plant extracts plus added vitamins such as Vitamin C, B-vitamins (niacin, pantothenic acid, biotin) and zinc. A 12-cube pack sits around €8.99, so roughly €0.75 per drink — clearly a premium over tap water, but positioned as a healthier, lower-waste swap for soft drinks and juices.

The crowd verdict is strongly positive and high-volume: 4.8/5 across more than 84,000 reviews on Reviews.io and a 'Great' 4.2/5 on Trustpilot from nearly 18,000 ratings. Taste, convenience and the reusable-bottle ecosystem are the standout wins; the main gripes are price and the fact that the classic Microdrink is more of a vitamin-and-flavour product than a sports electrolyte. For that, Waterdrop's dedicated Microlyte line (zinc, magnesium, potassium, calcium plus nine vitamins) is the better fit.
