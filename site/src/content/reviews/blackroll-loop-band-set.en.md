Short loop bands are the cheapest way to add glute, hip and shoulder work to a home routine. BLACKROLL's are fabric-covered, so they stay in place on the thighs instead of rolling up the way thin latex bands do.

Three resistances come in the set. They cost more than generic latex bands, and they only do the short-loop job: for pull-up assistance or presses you need long bands.
