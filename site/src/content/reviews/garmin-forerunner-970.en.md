Announced on 15 May 2025, the Forerunner 970 keeps the 965's 47 mm format and adds what that watch lacked: a sapphire lens and titanium bezel, a brighter AMOLED, a built-in LED flashlight, a speaker and microphone, ECG and Garmin's Gen 5 optical sensor. DC Rainmaker found land GPS and optical heart rate close to his reference devices, OutdoorGearLab scored it 83/100, and billiger.de lists an average of 4.5/5 across nine expert reviews.

New training tools include Running Tolerance (a weekly mileage ceiling based on load), Impact Load and a triathlon Garmin Coach; Running Economy and Step Speed Loss need the separate HRM 600 strap. The trade-offs are cost and battery: the RRP is 749.99 EUR (about 624 EUR street in Germany), and smartwatch mode drops to 15 days, although common GPS modes run slightly longer than on the 965.

Compared with our [Garmin Forerunner 965 review](/en/verified/garmin-forerunner-965), GPS, training suite and display move up, while battery and value come down.
