The Vyper 3 is a firm roller with a motor inside. The vibration makes it easier to stay on a tight spot, which is the honest case for it: people roll more when it hurts less.

The research behind vibration adding much on top of rolling is thin, and at €159 it is a premium product. In a studio, where getting clients to roll at all is the battle, it can earn its price. At home, a plain roller is the better buy.
