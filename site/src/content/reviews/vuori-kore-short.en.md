Vuori built its reputation on blurring the line between performance and lifestyle, and the Kore Short is the clearest example in the men's line. The fabric is a soft, quick-recovering four-way stretch that reviewers repeatedly single out as the most comfortable they have tested, and the built-in boxer-brief liner means you can skip separate underwear. It comes in 5", 7" and 9" inseams plus lined and unlined versions, so you can dial in coverage. User ratings are strong across the board – 4.8/5 from over 26,000 reviews on Vuori's own site and 4.6/5 at REI – confirming that most buyers love the feel and fit, which runs true to size.

The trade-off is that this comfort-first design is not a specialist running short. OutdoorGearLab ranks it as the most versatile but also the heaviest short in its test, noting it absorbs water and dries slowly, and the open pockets lack zips. Under heavy squats the liner can ride up. For German shoppers the important news is availability: Vuori runs a localised store at vuoriclothing.de with pricing in euros (€85) and free express shipping, so you are not stuck with US import duties.
