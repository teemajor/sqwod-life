Clear whey solves a real problem: a lot of people stop taking protein because they cannot face another milky shake. ESN's Isoclear mixes into a clear, iced-tea-style drink with up to 25 g protein in 30 g of powder.

The cost is the trade-off, at around two euros a serving, and it relies on sucralose and acesulfame K for sweetness. On Amazon.de it is sold through a reseller, so our link goes to ESN.
