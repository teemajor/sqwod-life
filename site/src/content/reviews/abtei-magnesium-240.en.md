Abtei is the unglamorous answer, and it holds up. One capsule gives 240 mg, just under the BfR ceiling, with no sweeteners and no titanium dioxide, and in ÖKO-TEST's June 2026 test it was among the six of 28 products that did not stand out negatively.

The compromise is the form: magnesium oxide is less well absorbed than the bisglycinate in Sunday Natural or PILLAR. At a few cents a day, that is a trade many people will happily make.
