The Gel-Nimbus 28 is ASICS' max-cushion neutral trainer, and this update is mostly about weight: ASICS says it is more than 20 g lighter, and RunRepeat measured 278 g. The lab results still read like a Nimbus, with high shock absorption, a stable platform for its size and strong grip. RunRepeat scores it 86/100 and RTINGS rates its cushioning 8.6/10.

The midsole foam has not changed, so energy return stays poor and the ride stays muted at faster paces. Testers also flag a narrow, low toe box. At about €140 street (RRP around €200) it is a sound pick for easy and recovery days, not a tempo shoe.

Compared with the [Gel-Nimbus 27](/en/verified/asics-gel-nimbus-27), the 28 is lighter and a little more nimble, with the same midsole foam and a similar fit.
