The Go 2 is Hyperice's small gun: quiet enough to use mid-conversation, light enough for a jacket pocket, with three speeds. Now that the Go 3 is out at €119, the Go 2 sits at €99, which is where it makes sense.

One German caveat: Stiftung Warentest found high naphthalene levels in the plastic of the full-size Hypervolt 2 in July 2024. The Go 2 was not tested, so we cannot say either way.
