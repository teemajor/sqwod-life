C4 is the pre-workout most people try first, and the moderate 150 mg of caffeine is a sensible place to start. The label lists every dose, at about 57 cents a serving.

The extras are where it falls short: 1.6 g beta-alanine and 1.5 g creatine are roughly half what research uses, and some flavours add Allura red. Think of it as a caffeine drink with a little support, not a full performance formula.
