The Cloudmonster 2 is On’s big, cushioned daily trainer, and the update genuinely improves the first: a wide, stable max-stack platform, a smooth rockered roll, and dual-density foam that adds pop. Independent testing lands it at 88/100, and it’s a durable, versatile cruiser for easy and long miles.

What it isn’t is soft or light. The “Helion” reads more like enhanced EVA than a plush superfoam, it’s heavy at ~292–300 g, and it runs large. For a coach recommending a durable cushioned trainer with On’s signature ride, it delivers — just set expectations on firmness, weight and sizing.
