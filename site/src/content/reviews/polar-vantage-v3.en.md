The Polar Vantage V3 is one of the most feature-complete training watches on the market. It pairs a bright AMOLED display and onboard offline maps with dual-frequency GPS, an optical heart-rate sensor, ECG, SpO2 and skin-temperature readings. Battery life is a standout, stretching to around a week in smartwatch mode, and Polar's training load, recovery and readiness metrics remain among the best for structured athletes.

Where it falls short is the everyday smartwatch experience: there's no contactless payment, music and app support is thin, and the software still shows rough edges that reviewers repeatedly flag. At the full €599.90 RRP it's hard to recommend over rivals, but around the €399 street price it becomes a compelling pick for data-hungry endurance athletes.
