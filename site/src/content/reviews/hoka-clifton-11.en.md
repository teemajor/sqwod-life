The Clifton is HOKA's best-known daily trainer, and the 11 keeps the midsole and outsole while changing the upper, sockliner and lacing. In the lab that adds up to a softer, lighter and more flexible shoe: RunRepeat measured 266 g and scores it 85/100, and RTINGS calls it slightly better overall than the Clifton 10.

What has not changed is the foam. Energy return stays very low, so this is a cruiser, not a tempo shoe. The heel is also softer and a little less stable than before, and outsole durability reports are mixed. HOKA's German RRP is €160, but retailers already list it from about €119.

Compared with the [Clifton 10](/en/verified/hoka-clifton-10), the 11 is lighter and softer with more room over the toes, at the cost of a little heel stability.
