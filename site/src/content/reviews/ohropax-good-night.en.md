OHROPAX is the name Germans use for earplugs in general. The Good Night version is mouldable silicone: you knead it soft and press it over the opening of the ear canal rather than pushing anything inside.

That makes it the most comfortable option here for side sleepers and for people whose ears are sore from inserted plugs. The trade-off is less blocking and a pack that runs out, about 8 pairs for €13.
