The ScanWatch 2 is the rare health tracker that looks like a proper watch: a hybrid analog dial hiding ECG with AFib detection, SpO2, temperature and up to a month of battery. For coaches with health-first clients, that discretion plus a genuinely deep medical suite is the whole appeal.

What it is not is a sports watch. There is no onboard GPS, the tiny OLED does little, and daytime heart-rate can wobble during hard efforts. Buy it for long-battery cardiovascular and sleep insight, not for running metrics.
