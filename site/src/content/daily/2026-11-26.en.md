The system uses that data to assess neuromuscular output, meaning how well the nervous system is driving muscle performance, as well as fatigue levels and whether the intended training stimulus was actually reached. It automatically detects when performance is dropping and adjusts the load based on objective data rather than estimates. Velocity-based load management has been common in elite sport for some time. milon is now positioning it as a commercial gym product, which gives studio operators access to that level of precision without building a bespoke setup.\n\nAnd finally, New Balance has signed a lifetime partnership with baseball player Shohei Ohtani, who first joined the brand in 2023. As part of the deal, New Balance renamed its innovation facility the Shohei Ohtani Sports Research Lab. Since joining, Ohtani has worked directly with the company's designers and sports scientists on performance footwear and apparel, making this more than a standard endorsement arrangement. New Balance reported nine point two billion dollars in sales for 2025 and has been expanding in Japan, where Ohtani is a central figure in the brand's global strategy. For brands and investors in the performance category, the deal is a signal that lifetime athlete partnerships tied to product development, not just marketing, are becoming a serious tool for long-term positioning.\n\nThat's Sqwod Daily for Thursday.
