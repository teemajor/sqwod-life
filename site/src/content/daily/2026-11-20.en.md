audioScript: "Workout Anytime has signed a deal to nearly triple one franchisee's gym portfolio across three US states, making it the lead story today.\n\nOn 19 November 2026, Workout Anytime signed a fifteen-club development agreement with Tri South Fitness, an investment group led by franchisee Steven Elliott. Elliott, a physical therapist based in Birmingham, Alabama, had operated eight clubs across Alabama, Mississippi and Tennessee. The new agreement takes his portfolio to twenty-three locations. The new clubs will open under Workout Anytime 2.0, a format that pairs standard gym equipment with premium recovery amenities. Elliott's group plans to open new clubs and also convert existing gyms. For operators and investors watching the value gym segment, this deal shows that experienced multi-unit franchisees are willing to scale hard when a brand adds recovery as a core offering alongside low-cost access.\n\nIn Germany, FitX opened its one hundred and fifteenth gym on 21 November 2026, its fifth location in Düsseldorf, in the Friedrichstadt district. FitX invested around two and a half million euros in a two-thousand-two-hundred-square-metre, two-floor facility with seven training zones, including a gymnastics area of more than one hundred and eighty square metres and a three-hundred-square-metre group exercise studio. The gym will run thirty-seven live classes per week, with taster classes starting 7 December and the full timetable from 21 December. That level of investment and class density from a large low-cost operator signals that competing on facility quality and programme breadth is now as important as competing on price.\n\nAlso in Germany, EGYM provided equipment and assessment technology for the Munich Ravens tryout day on 17 November 2026. The Munich Ravens are an American football club. The event was held at the Sportschule Oberhaching sports facility near Munich. The EGYM Fitness Hub measured body composition and squat flexibility, and EGYM Smart Strength machines recorded maximum strength after sprint and jump tests run by the coaching team. Existing squad members were assessed in the morning and prospective players in the afternoon, with results feeding directly into roster decisions for the 2027 season.\n\nAnd finally, around one hundred gym and studio operators gathered at the NH München Ost Conference Center on 25 November 2026 for FitNation München, a half-day event organised by and for the fitness industry. Sessions covered generating more revenue from existing members, reducing payment failures and chargebacks under SEPA direct debit, which is the dominant payment method across Germany, Austria and Switzerland, and growth levers including artificial intelligence, sales automation and medical weight loss. The former director of European affairs at IHRSA, now working in international business development at FIT-C, presented on improving revenue mix without growing membership.\n\nThat's Sqwod Daily for Friday.
