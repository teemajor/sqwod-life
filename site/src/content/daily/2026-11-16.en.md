Early conversations with investors have begun, though the plans have not been finalised and details may still change. The structure is called whole-business securitisation, meaning the company bundles its franchise income streams and sells bonds against them. Brands can book both physical and digital advertising formats across the entire network through a single point of contact. Studios join at no cost and receive a share of the advertising revenue generated. To put the opportunity in context, German government figures show that companies spent around two point seven billion euros on sports sponsorship in 2019, and gyms have historically seen very little of that money. For independent studio operators who are too small to appear in a national advertising plan on their own, a network like this offers a direct route into brand budgets that would otherwise bypass them.\n\nIn wellness commentary, Jessica Jesse, founder and chief executive of BuDhaGirl and co-chair of GWS 2026, the Global Wellness Summit, challenged wellness leaders to look beyond technology, longevity and performance metrics.
