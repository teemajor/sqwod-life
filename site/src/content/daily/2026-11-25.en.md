audioScript: "Club Pilates has rolled out a new studio design across its network, starting with a location in Foothill Ranch, California, where the redesigned concept debuted in July. The update includes new lighting, equipment, finishes, and redesigned lobby and retail areas. All new Club Pilates studios will open with the design as standard, and existing franchisees can choose to upgrade their locations. The chain is approaching its twentieth anniversary in 2027 and is the largest Pilates franchise in the world. With competition in the Pilates segment growing, operators running older studios will be watching whether the optional upgrade programme gains meaningful take-up across the network.\n\nNext, Midtown Athletic Club launched a strength training programme called FORGE at its Bannockburn and Palatine locations in the Chicago suburbs. FORGE uses XFormer equipment, which combines spring resistance, a moving carriage and controlled movements to keep muscles under continuous tension while keeping stress on joints low. The programme is included in the standard Midtown membership rather than sold separately. That model puts full-service clubs in direct competition with boutique strength studios that typically charge per class or require a separate membership.\n\nIn community news, Patrick Savage, owner of Savage WPL gym in Newcastle, County Down, held a ten-hour fitness challenge on 24 October 2026 called One Million Metres for Dementia. Participants reached the one million metre mark by lunchtime and completed over two million metres by five in the afternoon. The event raised ten thousand, one hundred and fifty-nine pounds for Alzheimer's Research UK, or eleven thousand, seven hundred and twenty-two pounds including Gift Aid, which is a tax relief that increases the value of charitable donations from UK taxpayers. Savage organised the event in memory of his mother, Bernadette Savage, who died from dementia with Lewy bodies. Structuring a fundraiser around a clear, measurable fitness goal gave participants a shared target and helped drive both turnout and total funds raised.\n\nOn the retail side, several activewear brands launched Black Friday promotions. ELV Active is offering up to fifty percent off its sets, and Beyond Yoga has cut thirty percent off all products, with up to seventy-five percent off sale styles. SPIbelt's promotion starts on 27 November. Alo Yoga, Aviator Nation, Vuori and Adidas are also running seasonal offers.\n\nAnd finally, Fitt Insider, a fitness industry newsletter, reported that listening bars, tea houses, book clubs and creative spaces are drawing attention as community-focused wellness alternatives to gyms and studios. The common thread is the concept of a third place, a space beyond home and work, where people spend time together offline. For studio operators, the signal is that members may return more reliably to a space built around community than to one built around a service alone.\n\nThat's Sqwod Daily for Wednesday. Thanks for listening."
