After that, he spent decades founding, investing in, and mentoring businesses across fitness, sports, and wellness. The induction takes place during the HFA Hall of Fame Induction Ceremony at The HFA Show 2027.\n\nNext, wearable fitness tracker company Whoop has reached a valuation of ten point one billion dollars. No further details on the funding round were disclosed.\n\nIn the United Kingdom, GLL, the social enterprise that operates public leisure facilities, has appointed Philip Donnay as chief executive and Andy Bindon as deputy chief executive. Both appointments follow the retirement of Peter Bundey.\n\nNext, BODY20, a franchise built around 20-minute whole-body electrical muscle stimulation sessions, says it is repositioning its model around strength and muscle preservation, particularly for women. Electrical muscle stimulation, or EMS, uses low-level electrical impulses to contract muscles during exercise. About two thirds of BODY20 members are women, most aged between 35 and 55 or older. Sydney Maddox, director of fitness at BODY20, says member conversations have changed. She says fewer women come in talking about getting smaller or burning calories, and more come in talking about staying strong, protecting their joints, keeping their bone health in mind, and being capable for the long run. For studio operators, this suggests that marketing resistance training around longevity and muscle preservation, rather than weight loss, may better match what this demographic is now looking for.\n\nIn Germany, studio software provider medo.check has published eight principles for what it calls future-ready studio management software, outlined in the trade magazine body Life. The principles cover customisation to individual studio workflows, avoiding isolated data stores where systems do not connect, a branded member app, and AI-assisted training monitoring through its medo.core product. The company also says its support line is staffed by people rather than automated tools, and points to more than 20 years of experience in training management and member care. For operators evaluating software platforms, the list offers a reference point for what to look for in a system that handles both administration and coaching support.\n\nThat's Sqwod Daily for Tuesday.
