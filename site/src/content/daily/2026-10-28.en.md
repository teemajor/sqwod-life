audioScript: "The DSSV, Germany's association of fitness and health club operators, is moving its 2026 annual event to Spain. The gathering will take place from 20 to 23 October 2026 at the Hotel Meliá Costa del Sol in Torremolinos, near Málaga. Tickets are priced at seven hundred and twenty euros per participant. The programme includes expert talks, networking, and Les Mills workouts. DSSV chairman Professor Doctor Thomas Wessinghage will host the opening reception on 20 October. This is the first time the event leaves Mallorca, where the two previous editions were held.\n\nNext, Nordic fitness operator SATS reported a twenty-two percent increase in third-quarter EBITDA before IFRS 16, which is a lease-accounting standard that changes how property costs appear on financial statements. The growth was driven by higher revenue per member and continued growth in group exercise, across a smaller number of clubs. For operators weighing whether to cut locations or hold them, SATS shows that a tighter portfolio with stronger revenue per member can expand earnings.\n\nIn the United Kingdom, Fit and Fabulous Somerset, a fitness group based in Burnham-on-Sea and Highbridge, raised nearly five hundred pounds for Breast Cancer Now. The group ran pink-themed classes as part of the charity's Wear It Pink campaign. Owner Charlotte Simmons said the total is still growing. Small studios can use charity tie-ins like this to bring members together and fill classes without significant cost.\n\nOura launched its Ring 5 in India on 22 October 2026, priced from thirty-nine thousand nine hundred rupees to fifty thousand nine hundred rupees depending on the finish, with pre-orders available through Amazon and Croma. The device is forty percent smaller by volume than the Ring 4, carries twelve signal pathways, and offers up to nine days of battery life. Its Health Radar feature uses artificial intelligence to monitor nighttime blood pressure patterns and cardiovascular trends, without directly measuring blood pressure. Accessing health insights requires an Oura Membership at five hundred and ninety-nine rupees per month. Operators building wearable-linked programmes for members in India should factor in that recurring subscription cost.\n\nAnd finally, Precor has expanded its Glutebuilder range with a new selectorized line. Selectorized machines use a weight stack rather than plates, making load selection faster and simpler. The machines were developed together with Glutebuilder and are designed to make targeted glute training accessible to members across different fitness levels. The move suggests equipment makers see dedicated glute training as a lasting commercial category worth expanding.\n\nThat's Sqwod Daily for Wednesday. Thanks for listening."
