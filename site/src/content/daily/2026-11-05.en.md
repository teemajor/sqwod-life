The most recent data shows the German fitness sector counted twelve point three six million members, nine thousand six hundred and forty-seven facilities, and total revenue of six point two five billion euros in 2025. OBSESS was founded by Tushar Sharma and Yash Kumar and brings together gyms, sports facilities, trainers, nutrition services, and fitness products on a single platform. The proceeds are earmarked for partnerships, expanding the platform's reach, and technology development.
