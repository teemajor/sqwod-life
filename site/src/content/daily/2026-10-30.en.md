That puts two affordable fitness brands under the same private equity owner, and it is a clear signal that consolidation in the low-cost gym segment in Europe is continuing.\n\nNext, Club Solutions reported on 29 October 2026 that Orangetheory Fitness and Crunch Fitness have both made wearable technology a central part of how their members train and stay engaged. Both chains use data from wearables to give members real-time feedback during workouts and to help their teams track progress over time. For gym and studio operators, this shows that feeding member-generated data directly into coaching conversations is now being used as a deliberate retention tool by large chains.\n\nIn sleep technology, Ozlo raised twenty-two million dollars in a Series B round. Series B means a second significant round of outside investment, typically used to scale a business. Ozlo makes Sleepbuds, earbuds designed for sleep that use Bose audio technology to mask nighttime noise. The company has passed two hundred thousand customers, supported by partnerships with Calm and Dreem Health. It plans to use the funding to expand into sleep conditions including tinnitus and epilepsy, and to build biosensing and artificial intelligence into its products. Fitness and wellness operators should note that consumer appetite for science-backed sleep tools is growing well beyond basic aids.\n\nIn Germany, Fitness First reopened its club at the Hamburger Stadthöfe in Hamburg on 28 October 2026 as the Fitness First HYROX Training Club. The site is built entirely around functional performance training. It offers guided HYROX classes, open gym time, and a recovery area with a sauna. HYROX is a competitive fitness race format combining running with functional workout stations. The fact that an established gym operator has converted an existing site into a dedicated HYROX facility reflects how quickly the sport is drawing participants.\n\nAnd finally, the aktivpark padel club in Kall, Germany, opened with three doubles courts and one singles court, all AFP Panoramacourts by Adidas and bookable online. The opening weekend included training sessions, a regional league tournament organised by the DPV, Germany's national padel federation, and an Americano tournament. Padel is a racket sport that combines elements of tennis and squash and is played on a court enclosed by glass and mesh walls, which are part of play.\n\nThat's Sqwod Daily for Friday.
