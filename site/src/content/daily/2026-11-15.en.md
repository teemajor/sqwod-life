audioScript: "YouFit Gyms has secured more than ten million dollars from Skyline Investors, a Los Angeles-based private equity firm, to refresh existing clubs and fund growth. The first phase covers twelve clubs in Florida, with upgrades to strength floors, recovery amenities and functional training space. This is Skyline's first investment in fitness and wellness. For value-segment operators, it confirms that private equity is willing to back club refreshes specifically to meet rising member demand for strength training and recovery.\n\nNext, Bodi, formerly known as Beachbody, launched Insanity: Unhinged, a 100-day home training program built around Hyrox and hybrid fitness events. Hyrox is a global race format that combines running and functional fitness exercises. The program is led by three-time Hyrox world champion Hunter McIntyre and executive produced by Shaun T, who created the original Insanity. Participants train seven days a week across 45 workouts in three progressive phases. Weekday sessions run 30 to 45 minutes, with a competition-style session on Saturdays. For gym and studio operators who have built Hyrox programming as a retention and acquisition tool, a well-resourced home platform entering that space is direct competition worth watching.\n\nIn the supplement space, dissolvable oral strips are gaining commercial ground in the 79-billion-dollar global supplement market. Sleep or Die raised one million dollars from True Beauty Ventures and built a 4,000-person waitlist for its bedtime strips. NuStrips secured shelf space at Erewhon and Urban Outfitters. Other brands in the format include Pemi for perimenopause support, Wizard Wellness for allergy care, Smart Strips using alpha-GPC for cognitive function, Nuet for gut health, and Riize, which delivers 50 milligrams of NAD+ per strip. NAD+ is a compound associated with cellular energy production. Fitt Insider noted that the claimed bioavailability advantage of strips over pills or powders is not yet proven, so operators and brands evaluating retail or partnership deals in this format should review the evidence first.\n\nAlso in equipment, LEVL Strength introduced a portable home strength machine that delivers up to 500 pounds of magnetic resistance with no installation required. Its isokinetic mode, which the company calls Iso-K Mode, adjusts resistance in real time to match the user's force output through the full range of motion. LEVL says an effective set can be completed in as few as five reps per exercise. For coaches and operators, the device is a signal of growing commercial interest in adaptive resistance technology outside a traditional weight room.\n\nFinally, Australasian Leisure Management Magazine reported that fitness operators are experiencing ongoing growth, while competition and member acquisition costs are also rising. No specific figures or parties were named.\n\nThat's Sqwod Daily for Sunday. Thanks for listening."
