Nuffield Health absorbs Hyrox into standard membership at 70 clubs
Nuffield Health, a nonprofit healthcare and fitness operator, has begun rolling out official Hyrox training programs across its UK health clubs. More than 35 locations are expected to offer the programming by January 2027, with 70 clubs targeted by 2028. The rollout includes classes, specialist coaching and in-club race simulations, all covered by standard membership fees. Nuffield Health has invested in dedicated training spaces, specialised equipment and instructor training at participating clubs to support the program.
For studio and gym operators watching competitive fitness formats, the key signal here is pricing. A large membership network is absorbing Hyrox, classes, coaching and all, at no additional charge to members. If this becomes the norm among major operators, it sets a price expectation that makes it harder for independent studios to charge a premium for Hyrox-specific programming. Operators building Hyrox offerings should consider how they will differentiate on quality, community or coaching depth rather than access alone.

milon launches Velocity Based Training system for strength equipment
Equipment manufacturer milon has introduced milon Velocity Based Training, a strength training system that measures the speed of every repetition to assess neuromuscular output, fatigue and whether the intended training stimulus was reached. The system automatically detects performance drops and adjusts load based on objective data rather than estimates. For studio operators, the product positions velocity-based load management, already common in elite sport, as a commercial gym offering.

New Balance signs lifetime deal with Shohei Ohtani, renames innovation lab after him
New Balance has signed a lifetime partnership with four-time baseball MVP Shohei Ohtani, who joined the brand in 2023 and has worked with its designers and sports scientists on performance footwear and apparel. The company also renamed its innovation facility the Shohei Ohtani Sports Research Lab, as New Balance reported $9.2 billion in 2025 sales and continued expanding in Japan, where Ohtani plays a central role in its global strategy. For brands and investors in the performance category, the deal signals that lifetime athlete partnerships tied directly to product development, not just endorsement, are becoming a tool for long-term market positioning.
