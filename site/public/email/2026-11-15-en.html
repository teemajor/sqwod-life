Connect the dots
YouFit's $10M+ refresh puts strength and recovery at the centre
YouFit Gyms announced a $10 million-plus investment from Skyline Investors, a Los Angeles-based private equity firm, to refresh existing clubs and support growth. The first phase covers 12 clubs in Florida, with updates to strength floors, recovery amenities and functional training space. The deal marks Skyline Investors' first move into the fitness and wellness sector.
For studio and gym operators, the investment is a signal that private equity sees member demand for strength training and recovery as a fundable thesis. If you have not yet audited your own floor layout and amenity mix against what members are asking for in those two areas, this week is a reasonable moment to start.

Bodi launches Insanity: Unhinged, a 100-day hybrid training program
Bodi, formerly known as Beachbody, launched Insanity: Unhinged, a 100-day home training program designed for Hyrox and hybrid fitness events. The program is led by three-time Hyrox world champion Hunter McIntyre and executive produced by Shaun T, creator of the original Insanity; participants train seven days a week across 45 workouts in three progressive phases, with weekday sessions of 30 to 45 minutes and a Saturday competition day. The move signals that home fitness platforms are positioning directly against the Hyrox training market, which gyms and studios have been chasing.

Dissolvable supplement strips draw $1M raise and 4K waitlist amid $79B category shift
Oral supplement strips are gaining commercial ground in the $79B supplement market, with Sleep or Die raising $1M from True Beauty Ventures and building a 4,000-person waitlist for its bedtime strips, while NuStrips secured shelf space at Erewhon and Urban Outfitters. Other entrants include Pemi, targeting perimenopause support, Wizard Wellness for allergy care, Smart Strips for cognitive function using alpha-GPC, Nuet for gut health, and Riize with 50mg of NAD+ per strip. Fitt Insider noted that better bioavailability for strips is not yet proven, meaning studio and brand operators evaluating this format should weigh efficacy evidence before committing to partnerships or retail placement.
Source: Fitt Insider →
05
LEVL Strength launches isokinetic home training system with 500 lb magnetic resistance
LEVL Strength has introduced a portable, adjustable home strength machine that delivers up to 500 pounds of magnetic resistance with no installation or fixed weights required. Its isokinetic mode, called Iso-K Mode, adjusts resistance in real time to match the user's force output across the full range of motion, and the company claims an effective workout is achievable in as few as five reps per exercise. For coaches and operators, the device signals growing commercial interest in adaptive resistance technology that removes the need for a traditional weight room setup.
