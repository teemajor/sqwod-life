✓ Track: Start measuring your 'community depth ratio': what percentage of your active members have attended at least one non-class event in the last 90 days? This single metric predicts churn better than visit frequency alone.
✓ Steal: Run a 'Members' Choice Charity Month' — let members vote on a local cause, set a collective class-count target, and donate a fixed amount when hit. Costs you almost nothing; builds belonging that no discount can replicate.
