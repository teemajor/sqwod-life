Connect the dots
Member churn signals appear months before cancellation
Data from Mariana Tek, a studio management software platform, shows measurable changes in member behavior three to eight months before a cancellation or downgrade. Among members who eventually canceled, average monthly visits fell from 6.2 to 5.4 in the final month before cancellation, and monthly spend dropped 11%. Julie Sippy, director of marketing at Mariana Tek, said the membership change is often the end of a slow, subtle disengagement trajectory, not the beginning of one. The challenge for studios is that disengaging members may still attend classes and generate revenue, giving no obvious sign that they are at risk.
For studio operators, this means a member who looks active on a roster may already be drifting. Tracking visit frequency and spend trends over rolling months, rather than checking current membership status alone, gives studios a larger window to reach out and strengthen engagement before a cancellation decision is made.

PureGym buys Swiss chain NonStop Gym for £235 million
PureGym is acquiring NonStop Gym, a low-cost gym chain with 47 locations in Switzerland, for £235 million. The deal is expected to close on 2 November 2026, after which NonStop Gym sites will be rebranded as PureGym, giving the combined network more than 100 Swiss locations by end of 2026. The acquisition follows PureGym's purchase of Blink Fitness in the United States in 2024, and will make PureGym the largest low-cost gym operator in Switzerland.

Global Wellness Institute flags EMFs as an unaddressed factor in built environments
The Global Wellness Institute published an article stating that electromagnetic fields (EMFs) are present in almost every indoor space but are rarely considered in design decisions. Citing research from the Building Biology Institute, the article links elevated EMF exposure to changes in sleep, nervous system activity, cognitive fatigue, and melatonin regulation. Studio and gym operators who are already designing around light, air quality, and natural materials may find EMF exposure a relevant next consideration for their environments.
Source: Global Wellness Institute →
04
J Potter Community Fitness raises £3,330 for Breast Cancer Now in one-week challenge
J Potter Community Fitness, based in Worcester, ran The Big Pink Journey from 14 to 21 October, with 100 supporters walking, dancing and exercising a combined 776 miles to raise £3,330 for Breast Cancer Now. The original targets were £1,500 and 315 miles. The campaign shows that a clearly defined distance goal and a one-week window can help small fitness communities significantly exceed fundraising targets.

ViralSpoon joins DSSV as a supporting member
ViralSpoon, an AI social media management tool, has joined the DSSV, Germany's association of fitness and health club operators, as a supporting member, announced on 21 October 2026. The tool connects to Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn and uses artificial intelligence to plan, create and publish posts automatically, matching a studio's existing visual style and tone. Studio operators running multiple locations can manage all accounts from a single dashboard, with campaigns adapted by the AI for each site so platforms do not flag them as duplicate content.
Source: DSSV →
06
Good Soul Hunting applies executive search methods to boutique fitness GM hiring
Good Soul Hunting, a fitness industry recruitment firm, is positioning the general manager role as a direct growth lever for boutique fitness operators. Co-founder and managing partner Matt Adey said a strong general manager or studio manager is responsible for turning a brand's vision into measurable results at the local level. For studio operators, the hire who runs the floor has more direct impact on member experience than any strategy set at head office.
