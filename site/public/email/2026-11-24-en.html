NHS pulls GP payments for Mounjaro: what it means for weight-management programmes
A report by Pulse found that most local health boards in England have withdrawn dedicated payments for GPs to prescribe and monitor Mounjaro, a weight-loss drug available on the NHS. Without those payments, GPs have less financial incentive to prescribe the drug, meaning patients who qualify may struggle to access it through primary care.
For operators running weight-management or GP-referral programmes that depend on Mounjaro prescriptions, the change could reduce the number of patients reaching their facilities through that route. If your programme is built around GP-referred members on Mounjaro, now is a good time to review how your referral pipeline works and whether it depends on a single access point.

Superpower cuts membership to $179 for Black Friday
Biomarker testing service Superpower, founded in 2023, is selling its membership at $179 for Black Friday, down from $199. The membership covers a blood draw across a network of more than 3,000 labs nationwide including Quest Diagnostics, a full report with 17 health scores, a personalized action plan and unlimited access to an AI-powered concierge; it is HSA- and FSA-eligible but not covered by insurance. For fitness operators and coaches watching the health data space, a blood-testing service that tests more than 100 biomarkers and flags early signs of over 1,000 conditions is now within reach of a wider consumer price point.

Ohana Growth Partners sells 7 Florida Planet Fitness clubs to Gulf Coast Fitness Management
Ohana Growth Partners, one of the largest Planet Fitness franchisees, sold its seven Florida clubs, totalling nearly 134,000 square feet, to Gulf Coast Fitness Management. Gulf Coast Fitness Management also holds development rights for 20 Planet Fitness clubs along Florida's west coast from St. Petersburg to Naples, plus rights for three new clubs in Broward and Palm Beach County. The deal signals active consolidation and expansion among Planet Fitness franchisees on Florida's Gulf Coast.

166,000 non-member visits recorded in 'United let's move.' campaign
The 'United let's move.' fitness campaign, reported by DSSV, drew more than 166,000 training visits from non-members during the European Week of Sport, which ran from 23 to 30 September 2026. A total of 1,950 fitness and health facilities in Germany and Austria, plus around 7,500 additional partner studios through aggregators, took part, and non-member visits rose 41.1 percent year on year, with over 3.1 million total training visits recorded across participating studios. For operators, the figures show that open-door campaign weeks can convert significant numbers of non-members into first-time visitors.
