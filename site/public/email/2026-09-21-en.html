Connect the dots
AI coaching just raised $5.96M — what's your moat?
General Catalyst doesn't write $5.96M Series A checks for fun. Disha's raise signals that the smartest money in tech now believes AI can sit in the coaching chair — not just hand out macros, but guide, adapt, and retain clients at scale. That's not a distant threat; that's a funded competitor with no rent, no sick days, and infinite patience.
Here's the rep that matters for operators: AI wins on availability and data; humans win on accountability, culture, and the moment a client is about to quit. Your moat isn't your programming — it's the irreplaceable feeling that someone who knows them is watching. Audit your client touchpoints this week and ask honestly: which ones could an algorithm already do better, and which ones only you can own?
