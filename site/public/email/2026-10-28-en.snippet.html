SATS Q3 EBITDA rises 22% as group exercise and revenue per member climb
Nordic fitness operator SATS reported a 22 percent increase in third-quarter EBITDA before IFRS 16 (a lease-accounting standard that affects how property costs are reported), driven by higher revenue per member and growth in group exercise, across a smaller number of clubs. The results show that a tighter club portfolio with stronger revenue per member can still grow earnings.

02
Oura Ring 5 launches in India from Rs 39,900
Oura launched its Ring 5 in India on 22 October 2026, priced from Rs 39,900 to Rs 50,900 depending on the finish, with pre-orders on Amazon and Croma. The ring is 40% smaller by volume than the Ring 4, has 12 signal pathways and up to nine days of battery life. A new feature called Health Radar tracks nighttime blood pressure patterns and heart trends without measuring blood pressure directly. Health insights need an Oura Membership at Rs 599 per month.
