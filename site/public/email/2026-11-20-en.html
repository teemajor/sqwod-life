The rundown
01
FitNation München draws ~100 studio operators on 25 November
Around 100 gym and studio operators gathered at the NH München Ost Conference Center on Wednesday, 25 November 2026 for FitNation München, a half-day event organised by and for the fitness industry. Sessions covered revenue growth from existing members, payment failure and chargeback reduction under SEPA direct debit (the dominant payment method in Germany, Austria and Switzerland), and growth levers including artificial intelligence, sales automation and medical weight loss. The former IHRSA Director of Europe, now Director of International Business Development at FIT-C, presented on improving revenue mix without growing membership.

02
FitX opens 115th gym in Düsseldorf with €2.5m investment
FitX opened its fifth Düsseldorf location, in the Friedrichstadt district at Herzogstraße 15, on 21 November 2026, investing around 2.5 million euros in a 2,200-square-metre, two-floor facility with seven training zones including a gymnastics area of more than 180 square metres and a 300-square-metre group exercise studio. Regular live classes, 37 per week, begin on 21 December 2026, with a taster class schedule starting 7 December. The scale of investment and the density of group class programming signal that large low-cost operators are competing hard on facility quality and timetable breadth, not just on price.

03
Munich Ravens used EGYM kit to test players for 2027 season
The Munich Ravens, a American football club, held a tryout day at the Sportschule Oberhaching sports facility on 17 November 2026, presented by EGYM. The EGYM Fitness Hub measured body composition and squat flexibility, while EGYM Smart Strength machines recorded maximum strength after sprint and jump tests run by the coaching team. Existing squad members were assessed in the morning and prospective new players completed the same diagnostic setup in the afternoon, with results feeding directly into roster decisions for the 2027 season.

04
Workout Anytime signs 15-club deal to grow Southeast portfolio to 23 locations
Workout Anytime signed a 15-club development agreement with Tri South Fitness, an investment group led by franchisee Steven Elliott, on 19 November 2026, expanding Elliott's portfolio from eight to 23 clubs across Alabama, Mississippi and Tennessee. The new locations will operate under Workout Anytime 2.0, a format that pairs standard gym equipment with premium recovery amenities, and Elliott's group plans to open new clubs as well as convert existing gyms. The deal signals that experienced multi-unit franchisees are willing to scale aggressively in the value gym segment when a brand adds recovery offerings, a combination that operators and investors in adjacent categories should watch.

Policy watch
SEPA direct debit failures flagged as a revenue risk in DACH markets
At FitNation München on 25 November 2026, a session addressed how to reduce payment failures, chargebacks and silent cancellations under SEPA direct debit, the dominant membership payment method in Germany, Austria and Switzerland. Studio operators in those markets were reminded that managing payment failure proactively is a material revenue protection task, not just an administrative one.
