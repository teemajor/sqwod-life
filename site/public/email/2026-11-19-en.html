The rundown
01
Triathlon App Stamina Raises $2M Seed Round Backed by Olympic Champion
London-based triathlon training app Stamina raised $2 million in seed funding, with backers including Olympic cycling champion Greg Van Avermaet, Belgian funds Syndicate One and 100IN, content creator Average Rob and former professional basketball player Tomas Van Den Spiegel. Founded by Willem Duyck and Shuowang He, Stamina uses a proprietary training algorithm built with coaches and professional triathletes to deliver personalized swim, bike and run plans, and has surpassed 50,000 downloads since launching in 2025. The raise signals growing investor appetite for endurance coaching tools that sit between expensive one-on-one coaching and generic training plans, a gap coaches and studio operators in the triathlon and endurance space may increasingly face from app-based competitors.

02
Therabody acquires thermal wearable IP from Embr Labs for women's health
Therabody acquired the intellectual property of Embr Labs, maker of the Wave wrist wearable that delivers precision heating and cooling to address hot flashes and sleep disruption, on 18 November 2026. Embr Labs, an MIT spinout, had sold nearly 250,000 devices before the deal; Therabody plans to fold the thermal technology and research into a women's portfolio that already includes PowerDot, its electrical nerve stimulation device with a period pain mode, building on a women's initiative it launched in 2025. For brands and operators in recovery and wellness, the deal signals that the menopause category is moving from passive monitoring into active, real-time symptom management, a direction also being pursued by Samphire Neuro, OhmBody and Eight Sleep.
Source: Fitt Insider →
03
Seca sales manager makes case for reframing body composition scanners on Club Solutions podcast
Ashley Ziegler Henzler, senior sales manager at seca, appeared on the Club Solutions Magazine podcast hosted by editor Taylor Gabhart to argue that gym operators should position body composition scanners as health analyzers rather than what she called 'fat scanners.' She said clubs serving wearable-literate and GLP-1 members risk losing those members to apps and AI chatbots if operators keep the conversation focused only on weight and body fat. Ziegler Henzler also described a scan day at a Charleston club where sessions with seniors and women asking about menopause surfaced new programme opportunities for the operator.
Source: Club Solutions →
04
McKinsey: U.S. wellness market tops $700B, growing up to 10% a year
McKinsey & Company's fifth Future of Wellness report found the U.S. wellness market exceeds $700 billion and is expanding at 5% to 10% annually. A survey of more than 10,000 consumers across China, Germany, the U.K. and the U.S. found roughly 40% say current offerings fall short in longevity, mental health and mobility, a gap that has persisted since 2024. For studio operators and coaches, that unmet demand points to categories where differentiated programming may find a receptive market.
Source: Athletech News →
05
ACISO Consulting raffles 12 months of consulting and online marketing
ACISO Consulting, a fitness industry consulting firm, is running a prize draw offering one winning club a full year of business development support and online marketing services. The prize follows two first-place wins at the bodyLIFE Award 2026, one in the consulting category and one in online marketing. Only clubs that are not already ACISO clients can enter.
Source: fitness MANAGEMENT →
06
Hone Health raises $228M to expand into peptides and concierge medicine
Longevity platform Hone Health announced a $228M raise on 18 November 2026, combining a $28M equity round led by MarcyPen Capital Partners and $200M in growth financing from General Catalyst's Customer Value Fund, adding to $33M raised the previous year. The company, which serves 200,000 patients through diagnostics, AI interpretation, and physician-led programmes, plans to enter peptide and GLP-1 prescribing, metabolic and autoimmune care, and a concierge membership tier that analyses more than 1,600 biomarkers. For fitness operators and coaches, Hone's move into peptides, GLP-1 prescriptions, and advanced diagnostics signals that well-funded digital health platforms are building clinical services that overlap directly with recovery, body composition, and performance programming.
