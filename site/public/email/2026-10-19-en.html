Mind Gym's 15% drop is a warning for every B2B studio
Mind Gym PLC just posted a 15% revenue fall in H1 FY27, and the culprit isn't their programming: it's corporate HR budgets getting quietly gutted. When companies tighten, wellness line items go first because they're easy to cut and hard to defend in a spreadsheet.
If your studio or coaching business carries corporate contracts, today is the day to run the math: what percentage of your monthly recurring revenue depends on an HR decision-maker you've never met? A concentrated B2B book looks like stability until it doesn't. Diversify toward direct-to-consumer memberships, retail, or high-ticket offers, and do it before your own forecast needs revising downward.
