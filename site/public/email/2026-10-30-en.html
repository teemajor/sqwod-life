Providence bets again on affordable fitness in Europe
Providence Equity Partners agreed to acquire Fitness Park Group, a budget-focused gym chain. Providence already holds an investment in VivaGym, another affordable fitness operator in Europe. The deal adds a second affordable brand to the firm's European portfolio and continues a pattern of consolidation in the low-cost gym segment across the continent.
For gym operators and brands selling into the affordable fitness category, the deal is a signal that private equity sees further scale and margin opportunity in budget gyms across Europe. Operators in that segment should expect continued competitive pressure as consolidated groups gain purchasing power and marketing reach.

Orangetheory and Crunch Fitness use wearables to personalise coaching and retention
Club Solutions reported on 29 October 2026 that Orangetheory Fitness and Crunch Fitness have made fitness tracking technology a core part of how members engage with workouts and measure progress. Both brands use wearable data to give members real-time feedback and to give their teams additional tools to support member goals. Operators in the sector can observe that integrating member-generated fitness data into coaching workflows is being used as a direct retention strategy by two large gym chains.

Ozlo raises $22M Series B to expand sleep tech and target new conditions
Sleeptech company Ozlo raised $22M in Series B funding to advance its products and target new sleep-related conditions, including tinnitus and epilepsy. Ozlo's Sleepbuds combine ergonomic design with Bose audio technology to mask nighttime noise, and the company has passed 200,000 customers through partnerships with Calm and Dreem Health. For fitness and wellness operators, the raise signals growing consumer demand for science-backed sleep tools, a category that is moving beyond passive aids toward biosensing and AI-driven solutions.

Fitness First reopens Hamburg club as dedicated HYROX Training Club
Fitness First reopened its club in the Hamburger Stadthöfe on 28 October 2026 under the name Fitness First HYROX Training Club, reorienting the site entirely around functional performance training. The concept combines guided HYROX classes, open gym time, and a recovery area that includes a sauna. The move signals that established gym operators are building dedicated facilities around HYROX as the sport draws a growing global participant base.

aktivpark padel club opens in Kall with courts now bookable
The aktivpark padel club in Kall, Germany, held an opening weekend of training sessions, a Landesliga tournament organised by the Deutscher Padel Verband (DPV, Germany's national padel federation), and an Americano tournament. The club opened with three doubles courts and one singles court, all AFP Panoramacourts by Adidas and available to book online. Padel combines elements of tennis and squash and is played on a court enclosed by glass and mesh walls, which are part of play.
