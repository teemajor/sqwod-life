Club Pilates rolls out new studio design as Pilates competition grows
Club Pilates debuted a redesigned studio concept at its Foothill Ranch, California location in July 2026. The refresh covers lighting, equipment, finishes, lobby and retail areas. All new locations will open with the design as standard. Existing franchisees can choose to upgrade, though the upgrade is optional rather than required. The brand approaches its 20th anniversary in 2027 as newer Pilates concepts compete for the same members.
For studio operators considering a refit, the Club Pilates rollout sets a visible benchmark for what a refreshed Pilates environment now looks like. The optional upgrade model means franchisees will be watching take-up rates across the network before committing their own capital. Independent Pilates studio owners face the same question: whether updated finishes and retail presentation are enough to hold members as the category becomes more crowded.

Midtown Athletic Club launches FORGE strength training at two Chicago-area clubs
Midtown Athletic Club launched FORGE, a strength training programme using XFormer spring-resistance equipment, at its Bannockburn and Palatine locations in Chicago's suburbs. The programme uses spring resistance, a moving carriage and controlled movements to create continuous muscular engagement with low joint impact. FORGE is included in the standard Midtown membership rather than sold as a separate add-on, a model that positions full-service clubs as direct competitors to boutique strength studios.

£10,159 raised for Alzheimer's Research UK in Newcastle gym's 10-hour challenge
Patrick (PD) Savage, owner of Savage WPL gym in Newcastle, County Down, ran a 10-hour community fitness event called '1 Million Metres for Dementia' on 24 October 2026, raising £10,159 for Alzheimer's Research UK, or £11,722 when Gift Aid tax relief is included. Participants hit one million metres by lunchtime and completed over two million metres by 5pm; Savage presented a cheque to Alzheimer's Research UK representative Hazel Stirling. The event shows how gym operators can structure a large-scale community fundraiser around a measurable fitness goal to drive participation and significant charitable income.

ELV Active, Beyond Yoga and SPIbelt launch Black Friday discounts
Several activewear brands launched Black Friday promotions, with ELV Active offering up to 50% off its sets, Beyond Yoga cutting 30% off all products and up to 75% off sale styles, and SPIbelt starting its deal from 27 November. Alo Yoga, Aviator Nation, Vuori and Adidas are also named among brands running offers this season, according to Athletech News. For studio operators buying kit in bulk or advising clients on gear, the Beyond Yoga sitewide discount and the deep ELV Active cut are the most clearly defined savings available now.

Listening bars and tea houses emerge as wellness third places beyond gyms
Listening bars, tea houses, book clubs, chess clubs, and creative spaces are gaining attention as wellness alternatives that prioritise community over performance tracking or health optimisation, according to Fitt Insider. These concepts centre on the idea of a third place, a space beyond home and work, where people connect and spend time together offline. For wellness operators, the signal is that people may return more reliably to a space built around community than to one built around a service or product alone.
