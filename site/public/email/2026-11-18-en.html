01
60-Studio Pilates Franchise @pilates 24/7 Plans US Entry in Miami and Dallas
Australian reformer Pilates franchise @pilates 24/7, which operates 60 studios across Australia and New Zealand and serves around 12,000 members, plans to open its first two US studios in Miami and Dallas in early 2027. Founded in 2021 by Tara and Russell Jarrett, the brand uses a tech-enabled model that gives members round-the-clock access to reformers via individual screens and headphones, with more than 700 on-demand classes on its proprietary platform, rather than relying on scheduled instructor-led sessions. The move signals growing international appetite for unstaffed, on-demand reformer Pilates formats, giving US studio operators a direct read on how that model may compete in the Florida and Texas markets.

02
Orangetheory trains 6,500+ coaches with NASM to support GLP-1 medication users
Orangetheory Fitness announced a partnership with the National Academy of Sports Medicine (NASM) to train its entire global coaching network of more than 6,500 coaches to support members taking GLP-1 weight-loss medications, calling itself the first fitness brand to do so at that scale. NASM brings more than 35 years of experience educating fitness professionals. With roughly 1 in 8 U.S. adults currently on a GLP-1 medication and more than half of those users saying medication alone is not enough for lasting results, studio operators who build coach expertise around this population may have a competitive advantage in retaining and attracting that growing member segment.
Source: Club Solutions →
03
YTTP opens its fifth Reformer Pilates studio in Berlin
YTTP, the boutique chain founded in Cologne, opened its fifth Reformer Pilates studio in Berlin on 14 and 15 November 2026, located at Backfabrik, Saarbrücker Strasse 36–38 in the Prenzlauer Berg district. The studio covers 300 square metres and holds 35 Reformer machines from the brand Your Reformer, distributed in Germany by Matrix. The opening is one of four studios YTTP plans to launch in the fourth quarter of 2026, a pace that signals growing investor and operator appetite for Reformer Pilates formats in German cities.
Source: fitness MANAGEMENT →
04
iFIT launches Tailor, a voice-based AI coaching tool for members
iFIT has launched Tailor, a voice-based AI coach that holds adaptive conversations with iFIT members, as a central part of the connected fitness company's strategy. For operators and brands watching the connected fitness space, iFIT is signalling that conversational AI coaching is now a core product investment, not a side feature.
Source: Athletech News →
05
Wellness Monster closes seed round led by Grip Ventures
Canadian kids' snack brand Wellness Monster closed its first seed round on November 17, 2026, led by Grip Ventures with participation from angel investors; the amount was not disclosed. The brand, founded by former Mars executive and ex-President of Love Child Organics Brittany Compton, launched in June 2025 and now sells allergen-friendly, nutrient-dense snacks including an 8g-protein Power Bites product across approximately 180 retail stores in Canada, including Whole Foods Market Canada and Metro. For fitness operators stocking or recommending kids' nutrition products, the round signals early retail traction for high-protein, allergen-friendly formats in the children's snack category.
