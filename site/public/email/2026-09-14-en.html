8-figure revenue, zero locations: the viability trap
A fitness chain doing $23M in annual revenue just shuttered every single Singapore outlet. Read that again. Not a scrappy startup, not a pandemic casualty limping along — a business generating serious top-line numbers that still couldn't make the unit economics work. Revenue is vanity dressed in a spreadsheet.
For operators, the lesson isn't 'grow slower' — it's 'know your number beneath the number.' Gross revenue tells you how loud the engine sounds; contribution margin per location tells you whether it's actually moving. If you can't rattle off your revenue-per-sqft, member acquisition cost, and break-even attendance per class by heart, you're navigating by feel in a business that doesn't forgive guessing.
