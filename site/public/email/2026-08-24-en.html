Sqwod recs
✓ Track: Start logging your 'tenure at cancellation' data point for every member who leaves — even a simple spreadsheet column. After 60 days you'll have a retention heat map that no NPS survey can match.
✓ Steal: Borrow the SaaS playbook: define a 'activation moment' for new members (e.g., attended 3 classes in first 14 days) and measure what % hit it — operators who track this cut early churn without touching pricing.
✓ Try: Run a 10-minute 'exit conversation' call instead of a cancellation form for your next five churned members — open-ended questions reveal objections your intake surveys never surface.
