Sqwod recs
✓ Steal: Run a 'life stage' survey to your existing client list — one question, three options (working full-time / winding down / fully retired) — then compare average session frequency and spend per group. The gap will tell you exactly where to double down.
✓ Track: Start monitoring your 90-day retention rate by age band, not just overall. Most studio software can filter this; if yours can't, export to a spreadsheet monthly. Retention curves often diverge sharply after 50 — knowing where the drop happens is the first step to fixing it.
✓ Try: Pilot one off-peak small-group format — capped at six people, built around mobility or longevity language rather than weight loss — priced at a modest premium. Off-peak slots that currently run half-full are your lowest-cost test bed for a dedicated 50+ product.
