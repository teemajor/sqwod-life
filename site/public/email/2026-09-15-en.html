Connect the dots
Record industry growth means nothing if your studio didn't grow
The 2026 HFA Global Report confirmed double-digit revenue growth across the fitness industry in 2025 — the kind of headline that feels great until you realize rising tides expose who's been swimming naked. If your numbers didn't move with the market, you weren't losing to a bad economy; you were losing to stronger operators in the same tailwind.
That's the uncomfortable gift of a record year: it strips away the 'tough environment' excuse and hands you a clear diagnostic. Pull your 2025 revenue against the industry benchmark. If you underperformed a double-digit growth market, the gap is your actual strategic problem — pricing, retention, programming, or visibility — and 2026 is the year to name it and fix it, not coast on the good-news headline.
