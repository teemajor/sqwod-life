Virgin Active agrees €135m deal to acquire Holmes Place Germany
Virgin Active has signed an unconditional agreement to buy Holmes Place Germany for €135 million, giving it an immediate foothold in Europe's largest fitness market. The deal marks Virgin Active's first major acquisition of an established cluster of health clubs. For operators and investors watching international expansion, this signals that large-scale club acquisitions in Germany are live at nine-figure valuations.
Source: Health Club Management →
02
25.6% of 60-75 year-olds able and willing to keep working, study finds
A study on what researchers call 'Generation Ü', people aged 60 to 75, was presented at the Federal Press Conference in Berlin on 5 November 2026. It found that 71.3% of that age group have good physical fitness and a willingness to stay active, while 25.6% specifically want to remain in work and have the physical capacity to do so. The study concluded that targeted prevention programmes could enable more people in this age group to stay professionally active for longer, a direct signal for studio operators building services aimed at older adults.

Life Time and Basic-Fit bring DJs and dance floors into gyms
Gym operators including Life Time and Basic-Fit are adding DJ hires and dedicated dance floors to bring nightclub-style energy to workouts, according to Athletech News. Life Time has posted DJ job listings and Basic-Fit has introduced a gym dance floor as part of this shift. For operators, the moves signal that in-facility atmosphere is becoming a differentiator worth investing in.

Connected gym equipment market forecast to grow through 2035
IndexBox forecasts the global connected gym equipment market will expand through 2035, with commercial fitness centers driving demand by using connected equipment to improve member retention. For gym and studio operators, the forecast signals that investment in connected equipment is becoming a mainstream retention tool across the commercial sector.
