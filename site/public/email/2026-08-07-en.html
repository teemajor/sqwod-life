One bad front-desk moment beats every retention dollar
A 62-year-old walked into Crunch wanting to use a treadmill and was told to 'get in shape first.' That's not a rogue employee story — it's a systems story. If your front desk has no clear mandate on who you serve and how, every person you hire becomes a brand liability operating on instinct.
The fix isn't a script, it's a standard: who is this gym for, what does every member deserve at the door, and how does staff know when they're getting it wrong? Your retention emails, referral programs, and social ads all assume the member got past hello. When they don't, the math breaks. Audit the first 90 seconds of a member's visit this week like it's your most expensive touchpoint — because it is.
