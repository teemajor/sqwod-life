Connect the dots
Club Pilates on growing to 1500 locations without losing what works
Tianna Strateman, brand president of Club Pilates, spoke with Club Solutions editor Taylor Gabhart on the Club Solutions podcast about how the brand is evolving as it approaches its 20th anniversary and 1500th location. The brand is introducing a circuit-style class format, driven by two signals Strateman identified: the rise of strength training and the growing use of GLP-1 medications. At the same time, Club Pilates is rolling out studio refreshes across its entire system, designed to limit disruption for franchisees and protect the member experience.
For studio owners, the approach offers a practical model: Strateman described how the brand chose which key performance indicators to track when launching changes across a large system, and how the physical updates were structured to keep return on investment clear for individual operators. The lesson is that innovation tied to measurable signals and tracked against specific metrics is easier to justify and manage than change for its own sake.

Global Wellness Institute: Lack of Belonging Is a Major Driver of Workplace Stress
The Global Wellness Institute has published a piece arguing that workplace culture, not standalone programs, is the foundation of employee mental wellbeing. It identifies lack of belonging as a large cause of workplace stress, alongside pressure from leaders, self-imposed overwork, and exclusion by colleagues. For coaches and studio owners who employ staff, the piece suggests that culture built by leaders matters more than any single wellness offering.
