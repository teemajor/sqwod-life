Arcana's multi-studio membership model launches in New York City
Arcana launched in New York City with more than 10 boutique studios on its network. Members pay $325 a month for nine classes plus wellness perks, booked through a single account. Partner studios include Reforming Pilates, The Training Lab, Nrthrn Strong, Sui Yoga, ID Hot Yoga, Güdsport, Nofar Method, The Silhouette Method, Good Day Pilates, Elahni, Serotonin Club, Powered on Bond, First Class Form and Outershed. Co-founders Felicia Dodge, who worked in analytics at Twitter and Visa and holds a master's in applied analytics from Columbia University, and Cole Tomlinson, a former mobile software engineer at Wayfair and Trello, started the company after working as yoga instructors and finding it difficult to train across multiple studios.
For studio operators, Arcana represents a multi-studio access product, similar to a network pass, that positions itself as protecting partner revenue rather than undercutting it. Studios already on the network gain exposure to members who might not have found them otherwise, while the $325 price point sits firmly in the premium tier, which may help avoid the margin pressure that lower-cost aggregators can create.

Kimberly-Clark closes $48.7B Kenvue deal to build wellness portfolio
Kimberly-Clark completed its $48.7 billion cash-and-stock acquisition of Kenvue on 3 November 2026, uniting 10 billion-dollar brands across personal care, consumer health, women's health, and active aging. The combined company projects approximately $2.1 billion in run-rate synergies from cost savings and revenue growth. For brands and investors in the wellness and personal care space, the deal signals that large consumer goods companies are consolidating around health and wellness at scale, which may reshape supplier relationships and competitive dynamics for smaller operators in those categories.

St Clement hotel in London combines medical, fitness and wellness under one roof
The Health Club at St Clement, the new London hotel from Soho House founder Nick Jones located at 180 The Thames, has launched with medical services, fitness and wellness integrated together, with Cleveland Clinic among its named collaborators. The pairing of a recognised medical institution with a hospitality-led fitness offer signals growing operator appetite for clinical credibility as a differentiator in the premium wellness market.
