The all-inclusive gym wave: blueprint or warning shot?
The all-inclusive model — one price, every service, zero friction — isn't a European quirk. It's a structural bet that members will pay more when they stop doing math at the door. body LIFE is reporting it's actively reshaping gym expansion strategy across Europe, and the operators paying attention aren't waiting for it to cross borders before they stress-test their own pricing architecture.
The concrete 'so what': if your studio still sells à la carte add-ons (nutrition, recovery, programming), you're asking members to make a buying decision every single time. The all-inclusive playbook removes that resistance by design. This week's question isn't whether to go all-in — it's whether your current offer can compete when a bundled competitor opens two blocks away.
