Pilates hit 13.8M — but which two groups own it?
When a category crosses 13.8 million participants, the smart operator stops asking 'should I add Pilates?' and starts asking 'for whom, and how explicitly?' Two specific demographics are driving this surge — and the studios winning aren't just running reformer classes, they're building language, scheduling, and pricing around those exact people. That specificity is what converts browsers into members.
Here's the pattern: mass adoption always looks homogeneous from the outside and is sharply segmented on the inside. The operators who identify the leading cohorts first get to own the positioning before it becomes generic. If your marketing still says 'Pilates for everyone,' you're already the backup option for someone else's niche offer.
