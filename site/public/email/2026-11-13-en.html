Men are now a third of Pilates participants: what it means for studios
Men now account for roughly one third of Pilates participants, a marked shift in a category that was once dominated by women. Studios including Club Pilates, STRONG Pilates, and solidcore are responding by aligning their programming and messaging to reach both men and women. Industry observers describe this as a structural change in who is walking through the door, not a short-term spike.
For studio operators, a broader co-ed audience affects class design, marketing language, and potentially pricing and scheduling decisions. Studios that have not yet reviewed their messaging and programming mix for gender inclusivity have a concrete reason to do so now.

WHOOP launches in-app social network for health and activity data
WHOOP launched WHOOP Humans, an in-app social network allowing its 3 million members to add friends, share Sleep, Recovery, and Strain scores, upload activities with photos, and comment on posts. The company says 57 percent of its members already belong to at least one Team, and members open the app eight times per day. For fitness platforms and brands, the move signals that keeping social activity inside a wearable ecosystem, rather than ceding it to Strava and its 200 million-plus users, is becoming a direct competitive priority.

HFA Names Greta Wagner Permanent President and CEO, Unveils Three-Year Plan
The Health & Fitness Association named Greta Wagner its permanent president and CEO on November 12 at Beyond Activ Americas in Dallas, after she spent six months in an interim role. Wagner led development of a three-year strategic plan, created with the HFA Board of Directors and industry stakeholders, aimed at delivering greater member value, strengthening advocacy, and expanding the industry's influence. For studio and gym operators who rely on HFA for lobbying and resources, the plan signals a more focused effort to make the association a stronger advocate for the industry.

Former Barry's and Grüns Execs Launch Studio Brand-Deal Platform PIRL
Ali Kaltman, formerly head of partnerships at Grüns, and Daniel Rabinovich, formerly VP of brand partnerships at Barry's, co-founded and launched PIRL on November 9, 2026. The platform matches brands with studios based on fitness discipline, member demographics, and geographic market, handles contracting and payments, and already counts Pvolve, SoulCycle, and Strong Pilates among its studio partners. Studios list for free; PIRL charges a fee on the brand side and holds funds until class attendance is verified before releasing payouts, giving studio operators a no-cost way to access brand revenue with a built-in attendance check.

Gantner launches face-recognition access system GT7 powered by XS4 Face
Gantner, an access control technology company, has released the GT7 powered by XS4 Face, a facial recognition system designed for fitness studios and health practices. The system identifies members with a brief look at a camera, requiring no card, smartphone or wristband, and is built to meet data protection and security standards. For studio operators, the technology can reduce front-desk workload, ease peak-hour congestion and support unstaffed or low-staff 24-hour access models.
