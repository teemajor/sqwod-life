Huberman joins Hims & Hers for up to $24M, studios launch fitness competitions & Groe names CCO
Your edge in the fitness industry. Five minutes, every weekday.
Andrew Huberman is joining Hims & Hers as a board member in a deal worth up to $24M, as studio operators enter the fitness competition space and Groe Solutions adds commercial leadership.

The rundown
01
Fitstop, Alpha Fit Club and F45 move into fitness competitions
Studio operators Fitstop, Alpha Fit Club and F45 are launching their own fitness competitions, entering a space where Hyrox has built significant consumer interest. Whether these operator-led formats can sustain themselves alongside Hyrox remains an open question for the sector.
Source: Athletech News →
02
Groe Solutions names Julio Marshall as Chief Commercial Officer
Groe Solutions announced that Julio Marshall has joined the company as chief commercial officer, a role in which he will lead commercial strategy, enterprise sales, revenue operations and strategic partnerships. Groe builds a gym intelligence platform that uses computer vision and management system integrations to combine member activity, facility demand and operational data in one view for multi-location health club operators. The appointment signals that Groe is pushing to connect that platform to senior decision makers across the industry.
Source: Club Solutions →
03
Swiss Re chief medical officer calls on insurers to invest in prevention
John Schoonbee, global chief medical officer of Swiss Re (Swiss Reinsurance Group), called on insurers to take a more active role in preventing ill health, citing worsening metabolic health and slowing mortality improvements as drivers of an unsustainable model. The call was reported by Health Club Management. For fitness operators, this signals that large reinsurance capital may be looking more seriously at prevention as a core business concern, not a peripheral one.
Source: Health Club Management →
04
Huberman joins Hims & Hers board in a deal worth up to $24M
Andrew Huberman is joining Hims & Hers as a non-independent board member, focused on science and marketing. Per a Securities and Exchange Commission filing, the deal includes $7M for two years of advisory work, $15M paid to Scicomm Media for three years of podcast advertising, up to $1M annually for optional short-form videos, and $50K cash plus $250K equity per year in board compensation. Hims is preparing to roll out sermorelin, glutathione, B12, NAD+, and L-carnitine, with BPC-157 and TB-500 to follow, signaling that brands are moving to position peptides as mainstream health products rather than niche biohacking tools.
Source: Fitt Insider →
05
JETSET Pilates uses AXLE platform to manage marketing across 150+ locations
JETSET Pilates has adopted AXLE, a technology platform serving fitness, wellness and beauty businesses, to coordinate marketing across its network of more than 150 franchise locations. The arrangement aims to give franchisees enough flexibility to run their own operations while keeping brand materials and campaigns consistent across the network. For multi-site franchise operators, the case points to the growing role of purpose-built technology in preventing brand misalignment as networks scale.
Source: Athletech News →

For fitness operators, this is a signal that large reinsurance capital may begin treating prevention as a core business concern, which could open new conversations around insurance-funded wellness partnerships.
