How HOTWORX built 913 locations by keeping operations simple
Stephen Smith, founder and CEO of HOTWORX, appeared on the Club Solutions podcast with editor Taylor Gabhart to explain the approach behind his infrared fitness franchise's growth to 913 locations. Smith credited a recurring monthly dues model, which he said has anchored fitness business models for more than 30 years by smoothing cash flow, and a philosophy he calls 'more workout, less time', which shortens session length to save members time and money. He described disciplined control of staffing, technology and programming complexity as the repeatable engine behind the brand's expansion, with 1,000 locations now in sight.
For operators and coaches weighing what to add, Smith's account is a direct argument that restricting complexity is itself a growth strategy. Keeping the member promise narrow and the labour model lean means each new location replicates the same proven system rather than inheriting the problems of a more elaborate operation.

DSSV study finds wide variation in fitness potential among adults aged 60–75
The DSSV, Germany's association of fitness and health club operators, released a study on 5 November 2026 at the Federal Press Conference in Berlin examining adults aged 60 to 75, known as Generation Ü. The study found this age group holds significant potential for continued work and social participation, but that health, social connection, and individual motivation vary considerably within it. For studio operators and coaches, the findings point to the risk of treating older adults as a single segment and suggest that programming for this demographic needs to account for wide differences in capacity and motivation.
