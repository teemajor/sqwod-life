Zing Coach brings AI personalised coaching to gym operators
Zing Coach Inc. built an AI fitness coaching platform that creates training plans based on a member's goals, available equipment, training history and schedule, then adjusts those plans using activity data and member feedback. Gym and studio partners can deploy it as a branded experience inside the Zing app, embed it through a mobile software development kit (a toolkit that lets developers add features to an existing app), or pursue a full white-label app. Independent reporting confirms the platform is already live with gym operators.
For studio and gym operators evaluating digital coaching tools, Zing Coach offers three integration paths at different levels of brand control. Because the platform uses ongoing member feedback to adjust plans, it can serve members with varying experience levels without manual reprogramming by coaches. Operators should confirm partner pricing and commercial terms directly with Zing Coach before committing.

TOUCH:BAY opens its gym display network to non-profit health campaigns
TOUCH:BAY, a digital display network that runs screens inside fitness studios, has opened applications for non-profit organisations, associations and initiatives focused on health and quality of life to run their campaigns at no cost. Eligible campaigns include projects covering prevention, mental health, physical activity, inclusion, self-help and support for people with illness; applications are open now and selection will be based on how well each campaign fits the network's themes. Studio operators whose screens carry this network can offer wall space to community health causes without any action on their part, which may strengthen ties with local organisations.
