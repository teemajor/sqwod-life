The beauty-wellness merger isn't a trend — it's a land grab
Amaani just pulled in $5M led by BECO Capital to scale a brand sitting squarely at the intersection of beauty and wellness. That's not a niche raise — that's investors betting that consumers no longer separate 'feeling good' from 'looking good.' The spa menu, the skin-health consultation, the recovery ritual: these aren't add-ons anymore, they're the category.
For studio operators, the so-what is this: your four walls of treadmills and kettlebells are competing against experiences that address the whole self. You don't need a skincare line — but you do need to audit whether your client journey ends at the workout or extends into recovery, ritual, and results they can see. The studios that figure this out first won't just retain clients longer; they'll command higher ticket prices.
