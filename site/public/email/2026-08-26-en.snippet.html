WHOOP just handed coaches a women's retention hook
Bundling Natural Cycles into WHOOP membership means female clients now get hormonal cycle tracking woven into the same device tracking their HRV and sleep — without you lifting a finger. That's a full-loop wellness story: train, recover, understand your body's rhythm. Coaches who speak that language in their onboarding and check-ins are suddenly not just trainers, they're the person who *gets it*.
The practical play here isn't to become a cycle-tracking expert overnight — it's to ask the right question during intake: 'Are you using any cycle-awareness tools?' That one sentence opens a conversation most coaches never start, and it positions your programming (periodized around energy and recovery phases) as something a generic app can't replicate. WHOOP did the bundling. You do the context.
