Connect the dots
Planet Fitness buys Portugal's Be-Fit for €102.5m
Planet Fitness completed the acquisition of Be-Fit, a Portuguese gym operator, for €102.5 million. The deal adds 25 clubs and approximately 80,000 members to the Planet Fitness network, marking a significant entry into the Portuguese market.
For operators and investors watching European fitness, this deal is a signal that large chains are actively consolidating the region. If you run studios or clubs in markets where a major chain does not yet have scale, expect more acquisition approaches and more price competition as global operators look to grow quickly through purchase rather than new builds.

Keybanc raises Planet Fitness price target to $68 from $65
Keybanc maintained its Overweight rating on Planet Fitness and raised its price target to $68 from $65, a 4.6% increase. The new target implies a 13.2% upside from the Planet Fitness closing price of $60.07 on 5 November. For investors and brands selling into the value gym segment, the upgrade signals continued analyst confidence in Planet Fitness as a growth stock.

GWI: global wellness economy hit US$7.5trn in 2025, may reach US$10.9trn by 2030
The Global Wellness Institute released research on 11 November showing the global wellness economy reached a record US$7.5 trillion in 2025. The institute projects that figure will grow to US$10.9 trillion by 2030.
