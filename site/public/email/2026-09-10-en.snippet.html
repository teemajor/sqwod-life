Connect the dots
Arketa's studio bet: your vendor is now your peer
There's a quiet power shift when a software company stops selling to operators and becomes one. Arketa isn't just testing its own product — it's now exposed to the same leaky pipes, no-show members, and Tuesday-3pm-dead-zones that every studio founder knows intimately. That's either a humbling education or a very expensive focus group, depending on how it goes.
For operators, the real signal is this: the tools you use are increasingly built by people who've never run a 6am class or sweated a payroll Friday. If Arketa's studio gambit works, expect sharper product decisions rooted in real operational pain. In the meantime, it's a timely prompt to audit whether your current tech stack was built by people who actually understand your floor — or just your funnel.
