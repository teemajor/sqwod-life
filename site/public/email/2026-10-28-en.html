SATS Q3 EBITDA up 22% on higher revenue per member, DSSV event moves to Málaga & Oura Ring 5 launches in India
Your edge in the fitness industry. Five minutes, every weekday.
SATS posted a 22% EBITDA rise in Q3 by lifting revenue per member across fewer clubs, while the DSSV shifts its 2026 event to Spain and Oura expands into India.

The rundown
01
DSSV annual fitness industry event moves to Málaga for October 2026
The DSSV, Germany's association of fitness and health club operators, will hold its 2026 annual event at the Hotel Meliá Costa del Sol in Torremolinos, near Málaga, Spain, from 20 to 23 October 2026. Tickets are priced at €720 per participant, and the programme includes expert talks, networking, and Les Mills workouts, with the opening reception hosted by DSSV chairman Prof. Dr. Thomas Wessinghage on 20 October. This is the first time the event leaves Mallorca, where the two previous editions were held.
Source: DSSV →
02
SATS Q3 EBITDA rises 22% as group exercise and revenue per member climb
Nordic fitness operator SATS reported a 22 percent increase in third-quarter EBITDA before IFRS 16 (a lease-accounting standard that affects how costs are reported) driven by higher revenue per member and growth in group exercise, across a smaller number of clubs. The results show that reducing club count while lifting revenue per member can expand earnings, a signal for operators weighing portfolio size against member yield.
Source: Health Club Management →
03
Fit & Fabulous Somerset raises nearly £500 for Breast Cancer Now
Burnham-on-Sea and Highbridge fitness group Fit & Fabulous Somerset raised nearly £500 for Breast Cancer Now by running pink-themed classes as part of the charity's Wear It Pink campaign. Owner Charlotte Simmons said the total is still growing. The campaign shows how small studios can use charity tie-ins to drive class attendance and community engagement without major cost.
Source: Burnham-On-Sea.com →
04
Oura Ring 5 launches in India from Rs 39,900 with AI blood pressure feature
Oura launched its Ring 5 in India on October 22, 2026, priced from Rs 39,900 to Rs 50,900 depending on finish, with pre-orders on Amazon and Croma. The device is 40% smaller by volume than the Ring 4, carries 12 signal pathways, offers up to nine days of battery life, and includes Health Radar, an AI feature that monitors nighttime blood pressure patterns and cardiovascular trends without directly measuring blood pressure. Access to health insights requires an Oura Membership at Rs 599 per month, a recurring cost that studio and gym operators should note when positioning wearable-linked programmes to members in the Indian market.
Source: News18 →
05
Precor launches selectorized line for its Glutebuilder range
Precor has expanded its Glutebuilder range with a new selectorized line, adding guided-movement machines that use weight stacks rather than plate loading. The machines were developed together with Glutebuilder and are designed to make targeted glute training accessible to members at different fitness levels. The move signals that equipment makers see dedicated glute training as a durable commercial category worth extending beyond specialist plate-loaded machines.
