Sqwod recs
✓ Track: Start monitoring your 'revenue per class slot' weekly — divide total weekly revenue by total available class slots, not just filled ones. Empty slots are silent margin killers most operators never measure.
✓ Steal: Borrow the 'brand as environment' playbook: audit one physical element of your space — signage, entry experience, locker room — and make it so distinctly yours that a member could describe it to a friend in one sentence.
✓ Try: Run a 15-minute 'churn post-mortem' each month: email 3 recently lapsed members a single open question — 'What would have made you stay?' — then look for the pattern across answers over 90 days.
